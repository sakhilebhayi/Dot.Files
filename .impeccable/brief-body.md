## Scope

The whole authenticated Dot.Files product UI (workspace, document page, approvals, trash, share
surfaces, operator queue, navigation, guest auth pages), Operate mode. Fourth and final direction
of 2026-10-31: the owner chose the Achromatic Ledger from four live full-system boards; the dealt
sheet (design-taste-frontend, seed 2063257168) is the law. The public landing page keeps its own
separate system and is out of scope.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The Achromatic Ledger — the owner's pick from four live boards dealt by the
design-taste-frontend app library (sheet seed 2063257168, kind workspace). A business ledger as
working software: no colour anywhere in the chrome, figures set in ink, the single data hue on
the one figure that matters. It refuses the Archive Room, the violet canon, and palette-swap
theming alike.

OWN-WORLD: The dealt ledger sheet, adopted whole. Blue-grey ledger ground #f2f4f7 (the chosen
face), white flat surfaces, 1px #e6e6e6 rules, NO shadow at rest. Ink #0a0a0a / muted #6b6b6b.
The primary action is near-black with white text; the secondary is a grey pill; destructive is
#c4242a. The single data hue #1d5fbf marks only the main data element (the "shared outside"
tally) — never buttons, never navigation. Status pills per the sheet (positive/warning/neutral
pairs). Geist for everything read (title 24/700, body 14, small 12, 700 otherwise reserved);
Geist Mono strictly for references and IDs; sentence case everywhere, no tracked capitals;
tabular numerals on everything that counts. Shell: no sidebar — a 48px top bar on the page
ground, logo on the column's left edge, plain grey text links. Dark theme derives the ledger at
night: inverted white-pill primary, hue lifted to #7ba4ec, same flat discipline.

STORY: A business person opens the ledger and reads it in a breath: the counts, the one blue
figure that says what is visible outside, and the next action. Nothing decorates; everything
reconciles — the product feels like well-kept books.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A 48px top bar on the blue-grey ground (real logo, plain grey text links,
company menu) over the workspace: slim company header over one hairline — Geist name, four
tabular tallies with 12px grey labels, "shared outside" in the single data hue when non-zero —
then the toolbar (search grows, filter, view toggle, grey-pill New folder, near-black Upload),
then the staggered shelf of snapshot cards. Primary action = Upload, near-black, top right.

FORM: Owner-chosen from four live full-system boards (achromatic-ledger, accent-ladder,
editorial-greeting, true-black-flat) mocked on real records and published as the Dot.Files
Design Board artifact; supersedes every earlier direction this day (Archive Room, violet canon
under seed 9140efee, landing-world monochrome). Taste-skill sheet seed 2063257168.

SIGNATURE INTERACTION: selection — hover/focus reveals a card's checkbox, any selection
surfaces every checkbox plus the quiet action bar (count, Clear, red-outline Request deletion);
.press scale on buttons and a short staggered rise as the shelf arrives (260ms, 30ms steps).
Reduced-motion kills all of it.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the
verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
